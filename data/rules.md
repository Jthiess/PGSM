# Server Rules

Welcome! These servers are a space for us to have fun together. To keep things enjoyable for everyone, I ask that all players read and follow these rules. I reserve the right to use discretion when handling situations not explicitly covered here — the spirit of these rules matters just as much as the letter.

---

## 1. Be Kind and Respectful

Treat everyone on the server the way you'd want to be treated. Rudeness, disrespect, harassment, or targeted negativity toward other players is not acceptable. Disagreements happen — handle them like adults. If something bothers you, bring it up calmly or reach out to someone rather than escalating.

---

## 2. Don't Take What Isn't Yours

Ask before you take — no exceptions, no matter how small. This includes:

- Items from chests, storage, or crafting areas
- Crops, animals, or resources from someone else's farm or build
- Blocks from someone else's structure, even "just a few"
- Anything that clearly belongs to or was placed by another player

---

## 3. No Cheating or Unfair Advantages (Minecraft)

The following are **explicitly prohibited**:

- **X-Ray** — any texture pack, mod, or method used to see through terrain
- **Freecam** — mods that allow your camera to move independently of your player
- **Hacked / Cheat Clients** — any modified client that provides illegitimate advantages
- **Macros** — automated key sequences or scripts that perform in-game actions on your behalf
- **Auto-miners** — mods or tools that automatically mine blocks without manual input

**Autoclickers are permitted** for repetitive tasks, provided they are not used in ways that provide an unfair advantage over other players (e.g., combat).

Beyond the above, use good judgment. Any modification that gives you a substantial, unfair advantage over other players in a way that undermines the integrity of the game is prohibited — even if it isn't listed here. When in doubt, ask before using it.

Approved examples include: OptiFine, performance mods (Sodium, Lithium, etc.), minimaps without cave-mapping, and cosmetic mods.

---

## 4. Rules for Other Games

The same principles apply across all games hosted on these servers:

- Be respectful to other players at all times
- Do not use cheats, exploits, hacks, or any unauthorized tools that provide an unfair advantage
- Do not interfere with, grief, or sabotage other players' progress or experience without their consent
- Ask before using or interacting with something that belongs to someone else

Game-specific rules may be added as needed. Check in with me if you are unsure about anything.

---

## 5. Enforcement

I use a 3-strike system. Once you get a strike, you will likely never get rid of it. If you are banned, you will never come back.

1. Warning: No action is taken
2. 2 Day ban.
3. Permanent ban.

The goal is for everyone to have a good time. Play fair, be a normal human, and everything will be fine.
